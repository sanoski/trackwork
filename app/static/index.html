The sidebar: lines, worksites, and jobs

The sidebar lists your work. Company lines track ongoing railroad work and can have worksites beneath them (a yard, a siding, a town). Sponsored projects have a tie goal and a deadline.

  • Tap a line to see the whole line, all worksites combined.
  • Tap a worksite under it to focus on just that site.

Whatever you select here is what the dashboard shows and what a report covers by default.

Log Work

Tap Log Work in the sidebar (entry users and admins). One screen logs a day:

  • Date: today by default. Any date works, so you can catch up on last week.
  • Ties installed: the day's total.
  • Of those, relay ties: how many of the total were reclaimed ties. Relay is always part of the total, never on top of it.
  • Track: optional. A day split across tracks is logged once per track with the same date; the second entry adds to the first.
  • Worksite: on a company line, where the ties went in. A day belongs to one worksite.
  • Equipment downtime: a row per machine, with the time down and the time back in service. The duration is worked out for you. Downtime can be submitted on its own.

Running totals and the comparison to the daily target are recalculated by the app. You never type those.

Correcting a day

Tap Edit on a row in the tie log or the downtime log. You can change the ties, the relay ties, one track's portion of a split day, or move the day to another worksite. Everything downstream is recalculated. Downtime entries can be edited or deleted the same way.

Manage: Switches & Derails

Each switch or derail is a named, dated record with a timber breakdown: for each length, how many were planned and how many went in, and whether that length is a head block. Tap + Add Switch or + Add Derail, fill in the name, date, and worksite, add a row per length, and save. Derails have a type: stationary (head block timbers), portable (no timbers), or switch (a switch-to-nowhere).

Diagrams show only timbers that were installed. Tables keep the full planned versus actual record. Switch and derail timbers count toward Total Timbers Installed but are tracked separately from the daily tie count.

Manage: Worksites

Company lines only. Add a worksite before logging work to it. Renaming keeps its history, because work is tied to the site's id, not its name. A worksite that still has work logged against it cannot be deleted.

Manage: Projects and Users & Settings (admins)

  • Projects: create a sponsored project or a company line, edit its goal, deadline, and daily target, and set its status. Exactly one project is active at a time; making one active moves the previous one to complete. Archived projects leave the sidebar.
  • Users: add the crew. admin does everything, entry logs and corrects work, viewer only reads. The last admin cannot be removed.
  • Email & Names: the organisation name on the report, the mail server, the From address, and who receives the weekly report. Send Test proves the mail settings.
  • Weekly Report: turn the timer on, pick the day, time, and time zone. It sends once per week, for the Sunday-to-Saturday week that just ended, and shows the next run, the last run, and any error. Send This Week's Report Now sends it immediately.
  • AI (Claude): create a personal token so Claude can log work for you. Each token is tied to one account, expires, and can be revoked.

Generating a report

The Report button shows the current scope beside it, for example Report · Full line or · St. Johnsbury. It follows your sidebar selection. Tap it to open the wizard. A live preview sits on the right (or below, on a phone) and refreshes as you change options.

  • Worksite: the whole line or one specific site.
  • Time period: the most recent reported week, a chosen week, the full project (first to last activity), or a date range.
  • Ties: New and relay, New only, Relay only, or None.
  • Switches / Derails: include turnout and derail timber work (company lines).
  • Itemize switch & derail (verbose): off by default, so all switches are summed into one "timbers by length" chart and one combined table, and derails likewise. On, every switch and derail gets its own diagram and table, which makes a much longer report.
  • Charts: weekly production, cumulative totals, work composition, and by-worksite bars.
  • Equipment downtime: the machine downtime log. It is line-level, so it turns off automatically when a single worksite is selected.

Type the recipient's email and tap Generate & Send. The email field starts blank on purpose.

Reading the report

  • Company line: leads with "Total Timbers Installed," then cards (switch timbers, switches, derails, new and relay ties, days worked), charts, switch and derail blocks, the tie log, and downtime.
  • Sponsored project: leads with goal progress (remaining, percent complete, projected finish), output against target, and the daily log.
  • Consolidated switches and derails: by default the report sums all switches into one chart and table and lists which ones are included. Turn on Itemize to see each one.

Other ways in

Everything on this screen can also be done by talking to Claude once an admin connects it, or from the command line on the server. All three write the same data. The Claude and command line guides are in the project's docs folder.
